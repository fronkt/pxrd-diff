1/12 = 0.0833 is the loss of a uniformly random guess on the torus. For t >= 0.25 the do-nothing
predictor already sits at that ceiling: x_t carries no information about x0.

Why: the coordinate noise std is sqrt(1 - alpha_bar(t)) in fractional units: 0.090 at t = 0.05,
0.167 at t = 0.10, 0.30 at t = 0.188, 0.39 at t = 0.25. A wrapped normal keeps exp(-2 pi^2 s^2) of
its first Fourier mode: 0.17 at s = 0.3, 0.05 at s = 0.39. So ~81 % of training timesteps
(t > 0.188) and ~40 of the 50 DDIM steps run on coordinates that are uniform noise. The schedule is a
unit-variance Euclidean schedule applied to a period-1 torus. For comparison, DiffCSP's log-uniform
wrapped-normal schedule (s in [0.005, 0.5]) spends ~11 % of its timesteps at s >= 0.3.

The origin shift changes nothing at the loss level: the dominant defect is the schedule scale, not
the origin dependence. Further defects found in the same read: sqrt(alpha_bar) * x0 is not defined
on the torus (x and x + 1 map sqrt(alpha_bar) apart), and the denoiser embeds absolute fractional
coordinates linearly (coord_proj), which is not periodic.
